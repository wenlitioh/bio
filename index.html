Biography

Born in Manjung, WenLi Tioh is a Malaysian tenor currently studying BA Music at Middlesex University. He has sung with LaVoce Choir, Yinqi Sacred Choir, YCA Chorus, and Nan Hwa Choir, with whom he earned a Gold award at the World Choir Games 2023.

In 2025 and 2026, he was selected as a Malaysian representative for the World Youth Choir. He has also appeared as a guest singer with the Malaysian Voices Collective and Rondofest, and performed alongside the Malaysian Philharmonic Orchestra. His choral leadership developed as section leader and president of the Mixed Choir and Men's Chorus at the Malaysian Institute of Art.

Besides choral singing, WenLi is an active violinist and serves as music director for the Manjung Chamber Music Ensemble.
